# Guru Parampara

## Purpose

Present the lineage with historical care, reverence, and accessible context.

## Content approach

- Establish a clear chronological narrative.
- Use biographies sourced from approved Ashram materials.
- Explain unfamiliar terms in nearby plain language.
- Separate verifiable history from devotional interpretation.

## Accessibility

Use a semantic ordered list or timeline that remains readable without animation or imagery.
