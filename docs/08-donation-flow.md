# Donation Flow

## Goal

Provide a respectful, transparent, and secure contribution experience.

## Flow

1. Select a cause or unrestricted donation.
2. Choose amount and donation frequency.
3. Enter donor and payment details through the approved processor.
4. Confirm the amount, purpose, and required declarations.
5. Show receipt and support contact details.

## Requirements

- Clearly explain where funds go.
- Do not collect or handle payment card data in the application.
- State tax and receipt eligibility only when verified for the donor's location.
