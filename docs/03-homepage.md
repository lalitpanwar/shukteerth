# Homepage

## Purpose

Orient a first-time visitor and guide them to teachings, an Ashram visit, seva, or a donation.

## Planned sections

1. Hero — invitation, primary path, current darshan.
2. Introduction — the Ashram's purpose and invitation.
3. Teachings — principal spiritual resources.
4. Ashram life — what a visit feels like and who it is for.
5. Visit CTA — dates, practical information, booking route.
6. Seva and donation CTA — ways to contribute.
7. Footer — contact, address, policies, and social channels.

## SEO intent

Target branded searches and high-intent discovery terms around spiritual retreats, meditation, and Uttarakhand Ashram visits.
