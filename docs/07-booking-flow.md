# Booking Flow

## Goal

Help an eligible visitor request or reserve an Ashram stay with minimal uncertainty.

## Flow

1. Choose dates or programme.
2. Review stay expectations and availability status.
3. Provide visitor details and requirements.
4. Review information and consent.
5. Confirm request or payment, depending on Ashram policy.
6. Receive an accessible confirmation and next steps.

## Requirements

- Preserve form data on validation errors.
- Provide inline error summaries and field-level guidance.
- Never represent a request as confirmed until it is confirmed by the relevant system.
